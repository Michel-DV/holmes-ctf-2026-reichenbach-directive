## How to use these pages

The case pages are intentionally concise. They provide a fast entry point into each investigation, its validation state and its relationship to the broader campaign. The full evidence narrative, task matrix, indicators, ATT&CK mapping and detection engineering material live in the [canonical master investigation](../reports/HOLMES_2026_The_Reichenbach_Directive.md).

Seven tasks remain explicitly open in the publication: Whisper Chain T8; Silent Passenger T13, T14, T19 and T20; Last Light T2 and T3. Their evidentiary state is preserved in Appendix B rather than being presented as solved.
